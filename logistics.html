Logistics

Your team stops typing. Starts moving freight.

Custom AI agents and automation for freight brokers, carriers and 3PLs. This is how it works.

What we automate

From inbox to TMS, without retyping.

01

Document intake

The agent reads rate confirmations, BOLs and invoices as they arrive by email and extracts load number, origin, destination, rate and dates.

02

TMS and spreadsheet updates

Extracted data goes straight into your TMS or the sheets your team already uses, so nobody copies fields by hand.

03

Customer and dispatch updates

The agent sends status updates to the customer and notifies the dispatcher when a load is ready to assign. Exceptions go to a person.
